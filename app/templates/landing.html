GOOD TO KNOW

A LITTLE
MORE CLARITY.

Questions about your vault? Start here.

What can I store?+

Personal details, JPG or PNG identity images, PDF, Word and Excel documents, and MP4 videos.

How do downloads work?+

Choose an encrypted copy to decrypt and download the original file.

How large can an upload be?+

The default request limit is 50 MiB, including upload overhead. The configured limit may be lower.

Which algorithms are used?+

The project creates AES, DES, and RC4 copies for coursework comparison. DES and RC4 are legacy algorithms and are unsuitable for protecting real sensitive medical information.
